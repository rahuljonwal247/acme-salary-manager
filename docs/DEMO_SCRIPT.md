# Demo video script (~4 minutes)

1. **Problem (15s)**: "HR manages pay for 10k people in 8 countries in spreadsheets. This app replaces that."
2. **Insights (60s)**: open `/`.
   * KPI tiles: headcount, payroll, median vs average (explain why the median matters).
   * Switch the breakdown between Country, Department and Job title. Hover a bar, then point at the table view.
   * Filter Country = India: the local-currency median column appears in ₹.
   * Histogram: the shape of pay across the org.
3. **Directory (45s)**: open `/employees`.
   * Search "sharma", filter Department = Engineering, sort by salary. Point out the URL changing, then
     copy it into a new tab.
4. **Employee (60s)**: open any employee.
   * Salary history with % changes. "Pay vs peers" band and compa-ratio.
   * Record salary change: type a lower amount with reason Promotion and show the rule rejecting it.
     Change the reason to Correction, save, and watch the history and band update.
   * Edit profile: changing department narrows the job titles. Show that salary and country aren't editable here, and why.
5. **Add + delete (30s)**: create an employee and land on their page with a HIRE entry. Delete with confirmation.
6. **Engineering (30s)**: the requirements doc, the layered backend, `npm test` (~1s) and the measured latencies
   in DESIGN.md.
